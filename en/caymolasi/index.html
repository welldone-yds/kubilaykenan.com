Pomodoro Çay MolasıFocus, let it brew, take a break

Pomodoro Çay Molası (Pomodoro Tea Break) is a calm focus timer that turns the Pomodoro technique into a glass of tea. Work for 25 minutes and the glass slowly brews; take a 5-minute break and the tea steams. Every finished focus session earns a sugar cube. The app is in Turkish.

What it's for

Watch the glass, not the timer

You sit down to work, phone in hand, and half an hour is gone. Çay Molası turns this into a calm rhythm: focus, a short break, and a long break after every four focus sessions. The glass does the rest.

Time in a tea glass

The glass brews while you work

When focus starts, the slim tulip-shaped glass slowly fills and its color deepens from pale amber to “tavşan kanı” (rabbit's blood), the deep red of perfectly brewed Turkish tea. No need to watch the timer; just look at the glass.

  • Swipe the glass up or down to change the time minute by minute
  • Focus 25, short break 5, long break 15 minutes; all adjustable
  • Every finished focus session is a sugar cube; four cubes earn a long break
Break

On a break, the tea steams

The break screen takes on its own color and the glass empties as you drink. Quotes with known sources accompany both focus and breaks: Seneca, the Turkish poet Yunus Emre, Lao Tzu and proverbs.

  • Countdown on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island
  • Notification when time is up
  • Optionally start the next phase automatically
Log

Every focus session goes in the log

Today's minutes, your daily goal, your streak, a chart of the last seven days and a breakdown by subject. You choose the subjects, such as lessons, reading, vocabulary or work.

  • Daily goal and streak
  • Chart of the last 7 days
  • Weekly time by subject

FAQ

Common questions

What is the Pomodoro technique?

A way of splitting work into short, focused blocks: 25 minutes of focus, a 5-minute break, and a 15-minute long break after every four focus sessions. Çay Molası shows this rhythm with a glass of tea.

Can I change the durations?

Yes. Focus, short break, long break and how many sugar cubes lead to a long break can all be changed in Settings; you can also swipe the glass to set the time minute by minute.

Does the timer stop if I lock my phone?

No. The countdown stays visible on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island; a notification arrives when time is up.

Does it work on Apple Watch?

Yes. Start, pause and skip from your watch; sessions finished on the watch go into the log on your phone. The interface is in Turkish.

Does it cost anything?

No, it's free. No ads, subscriptions or in-app purchases.
