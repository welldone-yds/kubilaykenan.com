HaritaKafaMap Türkiye in your head

HaritaKafa helps you study the geography of Türkiye on the map for AGS and KPSS: the Ministry of National Education's academy entrance exam for teacher candidates and Türkiye's public personnel selection exam. Don't memorize mountains, plains, dams and mines; see them on the map, find them and remember them. The app is in Turkish.

What it's for

Most geography questions are “where?” questions

Names of mountains, plains and dams read in a book are quickly forgotten when you never see where they are. HaritaKafa has you study every fact in its place on the map.

Who it's for

AGS candidates

ProblemGeography covers a lot, and time before the exam is short.

Solution55 topics in one app; each follows the same pattern: explore first, then find, then choose from options.

KPSS candidates

ProblemOn map questions, provinces, regions and landforms get mixed up.

SolutionFinding on a blank map, numbered map questions and 107 linking questions that connect a location with a fact.

People who review in short breaks

ProblemThere's no time for long sessions, but you need to study a little every day.

Solution60-second rounds, a daily goal, a study streak and an optional reminder.

Learn

Explore the map, tap, read

Pick a topic and the map opens with its items. Tap a place and read on the info card what it is and where it is.

  • Physical map layer, latitude–longitude grid, province names
  • Layered atlas: open topics on top of each other and read Türkiye as a whole
  • Add favorites and review them later
Find on Map and Which One?

Test what you see

In Find on Map (Haritada Bul) you tap the place that's named; in Which One? (Hangisi?) you choose the name of the highlighted place from four options. You pick the difficulty: guided, standard or blank map.

  • Find all 81 provinces, the regions and locations by latitude and longitude
  • Numbered map questions (I–V)
  • An info card after every answer, with the option to add it to favorites
Linking questions

Connect the place with the fact

107 linking questions match a place with its feature: which river flows where, which dam is on which river. Every question comes with an explanation.

  • Answers with explanations and a source note
  • See the place instantly with “Haritada incele” (view on map)
  • Exam rehearsal: 10 or 20 timed questions, submitted automatically
World

A world map of 197 countries

Find countries, Türkiye's neighbors, the Organization of Turkic States, straits and canals, capitals. Each country's card shows its capital, continent and relationship with Türkiye.

  • Neighbors and border crossings
  • Members of the Organization of Turkic States
  • Straits, canals and capitals

FAQ

Common questions

Do I need the internet?

No. All maps and questions are inside the app; it works on a plane or in the metro.

Are these ÖSYM questions?

No. The questions are original and written for learning; they have no connection to ÖSYM, Türkiye's central exam authority, or any other official body.

Which topics are included?

Locations by latitude and longitude, geographical subregions, landforms, rivers, lakes, dams, plains, plateaus, climate, vegetation, mines, energy, agriculture, industry, transport, tourism and more: 55 topics in total.

Does it work on iPad?

Yes. HaritaKafa is for iPhone and iPad; on iPad the map uses the wide screen. The app, including maps and questions, is in Turkish.

Does it cost anything?

No, it's free. No ads and no in-app purchases.
