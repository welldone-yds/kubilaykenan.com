SuflörLook at the camera, let the script roll

Suflör (Turkish for “prompter”) is a free teleprompter that scrolls your script right below the camera while you film. Your eyes stay on the lens, there's nothing to memorize, and the text moves at your speaking pace. The app is in Turkish.

What it's for

For everyone who talks to a camera

Talking to a camera isn't hard; remembering the script is. Suflör brings the text to eye level, so all you do is talk.

Who it's for

Film with the camera

Text that scrolls right below the lens

Open the camera and hit record. The text starts scrolling in a box close to the lens; when you stop recording, the video is saved to Photos.

  • Front or back camera, portrait or landscape
  • Tap the text to pause; tap again to continue where you left off
  • Change the speed with plus and minus as it scrolls (60–260 words per minute)
Your scripts

Write, paste, see how long it takes

Keep as many scripts as you like. Below each one you see its word count and how many seconds it takes at your chosen speed; making the text bigger doesn't change the duration.

  • Course intros, product videos, YouTube openings, meeting presentations
  • Word count and estimated duration under every script
  • Scripts are stored only on your device
Read on your phone

Full-screen prompter and mirror mode

For rehearsing without the camera, turn your phone into a full-screen prompter. If you film with teleprompter glass, mirror mode flips the text with one tap.

  • Large text, speed slider, countdown
  • Mirror mode: ready to go under the glass
  • Text size set separately for watch and phone

Suflör

Small details that know their job

Suflör focuses on one thing: keeping your text in front of your eyes at the right speed.

Scrolling on Apple Watch

The text scrolls on your watch too. Tap to start or stop. Digital Crown: adjust the speed while it scrolls, move back and forth when it's paused.

Record with your watch

If “Saatle” (with watch) is on in the camera, the text on your watch starts when you hit record.

Matched to how you talk

Relaxed speech runs at 120–150 words per minute; the default is 130 and you can change it.

Countdown

A short countdown before scrolling starts; set its length in settings.

Mirror mode

Flip the text for teleprompter glass.

No account

No sign-in, no ads; your scripts and videos stay on your device.

How many seconds is your script?

Suflör works out the scrolling speed from your speaking pace. Enter your word count and speed to see the duration.

This is calculated in your browser and never sent anywhere.

Apple Watch

The script on your wrist

On Apple Watch the text scrolls across the watch display. The Digital Crown adjusts the speed while it scrolls and moves the text back and forth when it's paused; press and hold to open speed and text size.

Privacy

Your scripts stay with you

Suflör doesn't connect to any server. The camera and microphone are used only while recording; Photos access is only for adding the video.

  • No account or sign-in
  • No ads, analytics or tracking
  • Scripts and videos stay on your device only
  • No in-app purchases

FAQ

Common questions

Is Suflör free?

Yes, completely free. No ads and no in-app purchases.

Where are my videos saved?

When you stop recording, the video is saved straight to Photos. Suflör never sends your videos anywhere.

How do I set the scrolling speed?

While recording, the plus and minus buttons change the speed by 10 words at a time. Choose the default speed in Settings.

Do I need an Apple Watch?

No. Suflör also works with just an iPhone; the watch is an optional second reading screen. The watch app needs the iPhone app to be installed. The interface is in Turkish.

Does it work with teleprompter glass?

Yes. Turn on mirror mode on the phone reading screen; the text is flipped and reads correctly on the glass.

Why does my watch go back to the watch face?

Open the Watch app on your iPhone: General › Return to Clock › Suflör › After 1 hour. Suflör then stays on screen for an hour.
