How it works

Open, finish, move on

Three steps: enter your target once, finish today's steps every day, see your progress.

1Set your target

Which exam, when, and what score you're aiming for. The days left and the number of correct answers you need are worked out for you.

2Finish today's steps

Vocabulary, questions, reading and grammar come mixed; each takes 5–10 minutes. The app sets the order.

3See your progress

Estimated score, accuracy by section and your streak. Your weakest section is added to the plan automatically.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

For anything else, see the support page.

Is it free?

Yes. The app is free, and all of its content (question bank, dictionary, reading passages) is open. No ads.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone, iPad and Mac. You can try it now through TestFlight; the App Store version is coming soon.

Where do the questions come from?

The questions, reading passages, translations and example sentences were written originally for Well-Done. ÖSYM's question format and difficulty level served as the model; no questions, texts or translations were taken from any book or publication.

Is the score calculation official?

No. The estimated score in the app is calculated from your number of correct answers using ÖSYM's scoring rules; it does not replace an official result.

Where is my data stored?

On your device. You don't need to create an account, and your study history is not sent to a server. Details on the privacy page.

Are you affiliated with ÖSYM?

No. Well-Done is not affiliated with ÖSYM, YÖK or any public institution, and does not speak on their behalf. The names YDS, e-YDS and YÖKDİL are used only to identify the exams.

Who made it?

Well-Done was made by English teacher Kubilay Kenan Işıklı (Kenan Hoca). It grew out of needs he has seen in the classroom for years: building vocabulary without forgetting it, getting to know the question types, and keeping the rhythm until exam day.

It is an independent project, with no ties to any institution, publisher or course.

Scores and calendar

Score thresholds and the exam calendar are compiled from public announcements by ÖSYM and other institutions. For applications, exam dates and results, always refer to osym.gov.tr as the official source.
