What it's for

Connector questions are solved by structure before meaning

On connector questions, most students translate the sentence first and then get lost among the options. Yet most options can be ruled out just by looking at where the connector sits and what follows it. LINK UP teaches this method step by step.

Four-group system

Know the group, rule out most options

Every connector belongs to one of four groups. Knowing its group lets you rule out the options that don't fit just by looking around the gap, without translating.

  • BO: at the start or in the middle, takes a full clause (although, unless)
  • O: in the middle only (but, yet, so)
  • İ: takes a noun (despite, because of)
  • ÇN: needs double punctuation (however, therefore)
Step-by-step solution

Structure first, then plus–minus, then the choice

Solve the question with technique: find the group first and the options that don't fit drop out; then check the direction of the two clauses, same or opposite; choose last. Your score is kept at every step.

  • Separate scores for structure, plus–minus and choice
  • Weak spots: the step where you lose the most
  • Solving technique: order of elimination, tense agreement, common traps

FAQ

Common questions

Is LINK UP free?

Yes, it's free. No ads and no in-app purchases. The interface is in Turkish.

Which exams is it for?

For the connector, transition-word and sentence completion questions in YDS, e-YDS and YÖKDİL. The technique also helps in other English exams with the same question type.

What is the four-group system?

A method that sorts connectors into four groups by where they sit in the sentence and what follows them: those that take a full clause at the start or in the middle, those that only go in the middle, those that take a noun and those that need double punctuation.

Which solving order does it teach?

Structure first, then tense, then plus–minus; meaning only comes in for the last two options. That way you solve the question without translating.

Do I need the internet?

No. All content is inside the app; your progress is stored on your device.
