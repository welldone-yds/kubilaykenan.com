Remembers your weights, suggests the increase

When a program day comes up, last session's weights and reps are pre-filled. Hit the top of the rep range on every set and it suggests adding weight; fall short and it suggests one more rep; struggle two sessions in a row and it tells you to back off a little and own the form.

Live workout

Set table with previous values, automatic warm-up sets, supersets, swap exercise, rest timer (on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island), plate calculator and a voice coach. New records are flagged instantly.

Body analysis

From height, weight and waist-neck-hip measurements: body fat, lean mass, BMI, FFMI, basal metabolism and daily expenditure. Pick a goal to get daily calories, protein-carbs-fat, water and an estimate to your target weight.

Nutrition and calories

Log meals from 350+ foods (USDA FoodData Central) or scan a barcode. Calories left, macros, fibre and water on one screen; 8 sample meal plans by goal; calories burned for 160+ activities via MET values (2024 Compendium).

A program made for you

Goal, experience, days per week and where you train: a balanced program in one tap, with sets counted per muscle and an RIR target on every exercise. Or pick one of 20 ready-made programs.

Supplement plan

25 supplements with current sources: what each does, evidence level, dose for your weight and when to take it, on training and rest days.

Progress

Muscle recovery map, weekly muscle volume, records, strength profile, measurement charts, monthly calendar and side-by-side progress photos.

Which exercise, and why that one.

The most effective exercises for every muscle, weekly volume per muscle, Apple Watch and a smarter next session.

Muscle guide

Tap a muscle on the anatomical map and the exercises that train it are ranked from S to C. The score combines stability, loading at long muscle length, target-muscle activation, ease of learning and fatigue cost, with sources listed in the app. The “For me” ranking adapts to your equipment, history and progress; add any exercise to your program in one tap.

Weekly muscle volume

Sets per muscle each week against a 10–20 set growth range, push/pull and quads/posterior-chain balance, and exercise suggestions for undertrained muscles.

A smarter next session

After each set you're asked how many more reps you could have done (RIR); if it was easy, the weight suggestion goes up. After the workout, rate pump, soreness and workload for each muscle and next session's sets adjust by one.

Apple Watch

Today's plan on your wrist: enter weight and reps with the Digital Crown, get a haptic when rest is over, record heart rate and calories. Results sync to your iPhone; complications for your watch face.

Strength profile

Your level in bench press, squat, deadlift and overhead press relative to body weight, from beginner to elite.

Muscle recovery

Which muscles are ready and which are tired, including runs, football or swimming. Mark a sore muscle by hand.

  • Sessions and packagesRecurring session times; remaining, planned, used and forfeited sessions in each package; payments and balances.
  • A file for every clientMeasurements, body analysis, program and supplement plan. See last session's weights before the session starts.
