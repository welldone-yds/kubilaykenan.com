FitDefter (iPhone) · frequently asked questions and contact

Found a bug, missing an exercise or have a suggestion? Write to destek@kubilaykenan.com. If possible, include your iPhone model, iOS version and the screen where the problem appears. English is fine.

Getting started

Should I choose athlete or personal trainer (PT)?

If you'll log your own training, choose "Athlete". If you have clients, sessions and packages, choose "Personal trainer (PT)"; you also get a "Me" tab for your own training. You can change the role later in Settings (profile button at the top left).

How do I change the app's language?

FitDefter is available in English, Turkish, German, Russian and French. Choose the language in your iPhone's Settings › FitDefter › Language; the "Language" row in the app's settings takes you there. Exercise descriptions, ready-made programs and supplement information also appear in the language you choose. Records you typed yourself (program names, notes) stay as you wrote them.

How do I switch between light and dark appearance?

In Settings › Appearance choose Light, Dark or System. With System, the app follows your iPhone's appearance.

The app opened with sample data. How do I clear it?

If "Fill with sample data" is on in the profile form, sample records are added so you can see every screen right away. Before starting your own records you can delete them all with Settings › Records › "Delete all records". This cannot be undone.

Training

How is today's weight suggestion calculated?

When a program day comes up, the weights and reps from that day's last session are pre-filled. If you reached the top of the rep range on every set last time, it suggests adding weight (e.g. +2.5 kg); if not, one more rep at the same weight. If you fell below the range two sessions in a row, it suggests reducing the load by about 10% and getting the form right. Suggestions are only a guide; you can change the values as you like.

What does the score in the muscle guide mean?

In the Muscle guide at the top of the Exercises screen, tap a muscle and the exercises that train it are ranked from S to C. The score combines stability, loading at long muscle length, target-muscle activation, ease of learning and fatigue cost; the research it's based on is listed in the app. The "For me" ranking adapts to your equipment, history and progress. The score is a general guide; your build and injury history matter more.

What is the "how many more reps?" (RIR) question after a set?

If you mark how many more reps you could have done (reps in reserve, RIR), the weight suggestion adjusts: if sets felt too easy, the weight goes up. If you rate pump, soreness and workload for your muscles at the end of the workout, the next session's sets go up or down by one. You can turn this off in Settings › Workout helpers.

The rest notification doesn't arrive.

Notification permission is requested when you tick your first set. If you declined, turn it on in Settings › FitDefter › Notifications. With Focus or Do Not Disturb on, notifications may arrive silently.

A form video won't open.

Exercise illustrations and descriptions work offline; videos stream from the internet. Check your connection. Not every exercise has a video; "Open form videos" opens a YouTube search for that exercise.

Can I add my own exercise or program?

Yes. You can add your own exercise from the exercise list, build your own program in the Workout tab or pick a ready-made one. "Create program" also builds a program for you based on your goal, experience and how many days a week you train.

Body analysis and supplements

How is body fat percentage calculated?

If you entered a body fat percentage you measured, that is used. Otherwise it is calculated from waist and neck (and hip for women) measurements with the US Navy circumference formula; without measurements, a rough estimate is made from BMI. Basal metabolism uses Mifflin-St Jeor, or Katch-McArdle when lean mass is known. These are estimates; repeating measurements under the same conditions (morning, before eating) shows the trend more accurately.

Where do the nutrition values come from?

Calories, protein, carbohydrate, fat and fibre per 100 g come from the US Department of Agriculture's public database USDA FoodData Central. Turkish dishes such as simit, menemen, lentil soup and bulgur pilaf were calculated from a standard recipe's ingredients using the same database; since recipes vary, they are approximate. You can add a food that isn't listed with "Quick add" by entering its calories and macros.

Why aren't calories burned added to my daily target?

Your calorie target is based on the activity level you chose in the body analysis, so regular exercise is already included. Adding calories burned again would count the same energy twice. Activity and workout calories are estimated with 2024 Compendium MET values and may vary by ±20–30%; they are shown for information.

Who are the sample meal plans for?

There is one sample day each for fat loss (1500, 1800, 2100 kcal), recomposition (2100, 2400 kcal) and muscle gain (2400, 2800, 3200 kcal). Totals are calculated from nutrition values; general guidance for protein, fibre and added sugar was followed. If your calorie needs differ, scale the grams; with illness, pregnancy or medication, consult a dietitian.

Are the supplement suggestions medical advice?

No. Supplement information is general information based on published guidelines (e.g. ISSN, NIH ODS); the app does not sell supplements. If you have a health condition, take medication, or are pregnant or breastfeeding, talk to your doctor.

Personal trainers

What is a "forfeited session" in a package?

A session the client missed or cancelled late; it is deducted from the package. The package card shows remaining, planned, done and forfeited sessions separately. A session cancelled in time is not deducted.

Can my client see their program on their own phone?

Yes, with a file. Use "Send program file" in the program details to send the program via WhatsApp or AirDrop; when your client opens the file in FitDefter, the program is added to their programs. Your client can also send you a finished workout as a file. Since there is no account or server, the two phones don't sync automatically. You can also share a monthly PDF report and a short status from the client's menu.

My data

Where is my data, and what happens when I change phones?

All records are on your phone; there is no account or server. When changing phones you have three options: transfer your iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup; make a single-file backup in Settings › Backup & export and restore it on the new phone; or turn on optional sync in Settings › iCloud. You can also export your workout history as CSV (Excel) from the same place. If you delete the app, the records on the phone are deleted. Details: privacy policy.
