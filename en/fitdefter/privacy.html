Privacy policy

Effective date: 26.11.2026 (1.2) · FitDefter (iPhone) · Developer: Kubilay Kenan Işıklı

In short: FitDefter collects no personal data. Your workouts, measurements, body analysis details, meal, water and activity logs, supplement plan and (if you're a personal trainer) client records are stored only on your phone. There is no account, server, advertising, analytics or tracking; nobody, including the developer, can access your data.

Where is your data?

Everything you enter in the app (workouts and sets, programs, measurements, height, weight and body analysis inputs, meal, water and activity logs, supplement plan and check-offs, progress photos) stays in the app's database on your phone. Data is not sent to the developer or to any server. If you delete the app, these records are deleted too.

iCloud sync (optional, off by default): if you turn it on in Settings › iCloud, your data is stored in the private area of your own iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit service and synced across iPhones and iPads signed in to the same Apple Account. This area belongs only to you; nobody, including the developer, can access it. Apple's iCloud terms apply. If you turn it off, your data stays on your phone.

Backup file: if you tap "Back up" in Settings › Backup & export, all your data is written to a single file (.fitdefter) and you choose where to save it (e.g. iCloud Drive or Files). The file stays only where you put it; FitDefter does not send it to any server. If you tap "Restore from backup", your current data is not deleted but moved to a separate folder on your phone.

CSV export: from Settings › Backup & export or (if you're a trainer) from a client's menu you can share your workout history as a .csv file. The file goes only to the app or person you choose; FitDefter itself sends it nowhere.

Weekly check-in (trainers): your clients' sleep, nutrition, energy, stress and weight ratings are stored only on your phone (or in your own iCloud account if you turned on iCloud sync). If you tap "Send questions via WhatsApp", WhatsApp opens with a message containing the questions; you do the sending.

If your iPhone's own backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) is on, iOS may include the app's data in that system backup. This is Apple's device backup and is subject to Apple's terms; FitDefter sends nothing anywhere itself.

For personal trainers: client information

In the trainer role, the information you enter for your clients (name, optional phone number, goal, session times, packages and payments, measurements, body analysis, nutrition logs, program, supplement plan, photo) is stored only on your phone; it is not sent to your client or anyone else. If you choose to share a report or supplement plan via WhatsApp, the text is sent from WhatsApp's own screen with your confirmation. It is your responsibility to inform your clients when you record their information.

Data we collect

None. The app has no account or sign-in. No usage statistics, crash-reporting tool, advertising identifier, location or any other information is collected. The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking tools (SDKs). Privacy label on the App Store: "Data Not Collected".

Apple Health (HealthKit)

The Apple Health connection is optional and off by default; if you turn it on in Settings › Apple Health, iOS asks for permission and you choose which data to allow.

  • Write: if on, your own workouts finished in FitDefter (type, start and end time, estimated calories and distance where available) are written to Health as workouts. Client records are not written to Health.
  • Read: if on, workouts from the last 30 days recorded by other apps or your watch (type, duration, distance) are read from Health and added to FitDefter as records. Last night's sleep duration, heart rate variability (HRV) and resting heart rate are also read to calculate a "readiness score" on the phone only; these values are not stored, only the score is cached for that day.
  • Apple Watch: during a workout on your watch, heart rate and calories burned are measured on the watch; the watch writes the workout to Health and sends its summary (sets, duration) to FitDefter on your phone.

Data read from Health is stored only in FitDefter's database on your phone; it is not used for advertising, marketing or data mining, not given to third parties, not sold, and not sent to iCloud or any other server. You can turn the connection off at any time in FitDefter Settings or on your iPhone under Health › Sharing › Apps › FitDefter.

What permissions are for

No permission is requested at first launch; each one is asked the first time you use the related feature. The rest of the app works even if you decline. You can turn permissions off at any time in Settings › FitDefter.

Internet connections

The app works offline; exercise illustrations and descriptions are built in. It connects to the internet only when you ask:

  • Form videos: if you tap "Watch video" on an exercise, the video is streamed from Pexels or wger.de. As with any web request, those sites may see standard connection information such as your IP address; the app sends them no personal data.
  • Adding food by barcode: when you scan a product barcode, only the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts (an open food database, openfoodfacts.org) to fetch the product's nutrition values. Nothing else is sent; Open Food Facts' own privacy terms apply.
  • YouTube search and sources: "Open form videos" and supplement source links open in Safari. Those sites' own privacy terms apply.

Health information note

Information on the body analysis, calorie and supplement screens is for general guidance and does not replace medical diagnosis, treatment or dietitian advice. It is also calculated and stored only on your phone.

Children

FitDefter asks no one for personal information and does not collect it from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the current text is published on this page and the effective date is updated. If a change would start collecting data, it will first be clearly announced in the app.
