    About

    I hold a degree in English Language Teaching and work as an English teacher at Ankara Dil Akademisi, where my job is to turn scattered preparation for YDS and YÖKDİL into a plan students can actually follow.

    FAQ

    Questions you might have

    If it's not here, write by e-mail or WhatsApp.

    01Is Well-Done free?

    Yes. You can try it on TestFlight now; it is coming to the App Store very soon.

    02Which devices does it run on?

    iPhone, iPad and Mac. There is no Android version yet.

    03Where do the questions come from?

    All 931 are original questions written in the ÖSYM format, each with an explanation of how to solve it.

    04Does the app collect my data?

    No. You don't need an account and your progress stays on your device. See the Well-Done privacy page for details.

    05Where do the dictionary and word of the day come from?

    From the same 1,388-word dictionary as Well-Done, with meanings, synonyms, example sentences and past ÖSYM exams.

    06How accurate is the score calculator?

    Each correct answer in YDS is worth 1.25 points; the calculator turns your correct answers into a score and a letter level. Thresholds come from official sources; check the institution's latest notice before applying.

    07How can I keep track of exam dates?

    Tap "Add to my calendar" once: exam and application dates appear in your calendar and update if ÖSYM changes them.

    08Who is Bibi?

    The mascot of Well-Done and this site: a little study buddy with a speech-bubble body and a graduation cap. It hosts the word duel and cheers you on in the tests.

    09Can I install the site on my phone?

    Yes. On Android, open it in Chrome and tap "Install on your phone" or "Add to Home screen"; on iPhone, use Safari › Share › Add to Home Screen. The dictionary, duel, level test and score calculator then work offline. On iPhone, iPad and Mac, Well-Done offers much more.

    10Can I use the PDFs?

    Yes, they are all free to download, print and share with your students.

    11How do I report a mistake or suggest something?

    Write to iletisim@kubilaykenan.com or reach me on WhatsApp. I read every note.
