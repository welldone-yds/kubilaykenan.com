About

I hold a degree in English Language Teaching and work as an English teacher at Ankara Dil Akademisi, where my job is to turn scattered preparation for YDS and YÖKDİL into a plan students can actually follow.

I have taught at middle and high school level, worked as an educational content editor and volunteered as a translator at an international sports event (FIS). I spent a semester in Poland on the Erasmus+ exchange programme and completed Pearson's PTE Trainer: Foundation course.

What I do

  • Setting target scores and weekly study plans in one-to-one meetings
  • Tracking progress with regular records and adjusting the plan when needed
  • Preparing vocabulary, grammar and reading materials in YDS and YÖKDİL format
  • Creating academic English content on the “Kenan Hoca” accounts
