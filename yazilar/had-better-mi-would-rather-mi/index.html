    Had better mı, would rather mı? Tavsiye ile tercih

    20 Ekim 2026 · 1 dk okuma · Kubilay Kenan Işıklı (Kenan Hoca)

    İkisi de kısaltılınca 'd olur (you'd better, I'd rather); bu yüzden karışır. Anlamları ve kalıpları ayrıdır.

    Had better: "-se iyi olur"

    had better + V1 uyarı ve tavsiye bildirir; genellikle belirli, o anki bir durum için kullanılır.

    • You had better leave now, or you will miss the train.
    • Olumsuzu: You had better not be late again.

    Would rather: "-meyi tercih ederim"

    would rather + V1 (+ than + V1) tercih bildirir.

    • I would rather stay at home than go out tonight.
    • Olumsuzu: I would rather not talk about it.

    İkisinde de "to" yok

    • Yanlış: You had better to stay. · I would rather to walk.
    • Doğru: You had better stay. · I would rather walk.

    'd hangisi?

    'd hem had hem would olabilir. Arkasından better geliyorsa had, rather geliyorsa would: You'd better hurry. · I'd rather wait.

    Kendini dene

    1. It is getting dark. We ---- go back now.
    2. I ---- walk than take the bus.
    3. You had better ---- inside today. (to stay / stay)

    Cevaplar: 1. had better · 2. would rather · 3. stay
