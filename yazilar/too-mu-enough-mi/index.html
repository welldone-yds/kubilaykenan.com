Too mu, enough mı? Kalıp da yer de farklı

20 Ekim 2026 · 1 dk okuma · Kubilay Kenan Işıklı (Kenan Hoca)

Too ve enough YDS'de hem anlamıyla hem de cümledeki yeriyle sorulur. İkisi de "to + V1" ile kullanılabilir ama verdikleri sonuç birbirinin tersidir.

Too: yapılamayacak kadar fazla

too + sıfat/zarf (+ for someone) + to V1

  • The text was too complex to translate. (Çevrilemeyecek kadar karmaşıktı.)
  • The box was too heavy for her to carry.

Too olumsuz bir sonuç ima eder: "o kadar … ki yapılamaz". Bu yüzden very ile aynı şey değildir:

  • The exam was very difficult, but I passed.
  • The exam was too difficult for me to pass.

Enough: yapılabilecek kadar yeterli

sıfat/zarf + enough + to V1

  • The text was simple enough to translate. (Çevrilebilecek kadar basitti.)
  • He isn't experienced enough to lead the team.

Yerine dikkat

  • Enough sıfattan ve zarftan sonra gelir: experienced enough, fast enough.
  • Enough isimden önce gelir: enough time, enough money.
  • İsimle "fazla" anlamı için too much / too many kullanılır: too much noise, too many mistakes.

Kendini dene

  1. He isn't ---- to apply for the position. (experienced enough / enough experienced)
  2. The box was ---- heavy for her to carry. (too / enough)
  3. We don't have ---- to finish the project today. (time enough / enough time)

Cevaplar: 1. experienced enough · 2. too · 3. enough time
